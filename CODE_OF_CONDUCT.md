# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

* Being respectful
* Being constructive
* Accepting feedback
* Helping others

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* Harassment
* Personal attacks
* Discrimination
* Trolling or abusive language

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct and may remove content or contributors who violate these standards.

By participating in this project, you agree to abide by this Code of Conduct.
